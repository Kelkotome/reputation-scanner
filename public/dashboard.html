Case dashboard

Manage follow-ups and prepare a branded summary for the police, a law firm or your PR adviser.

Email follow-ups

One follow-up is due 14 days after the provider accepts the first email. Record a reply or close the request here to stop a queued follow-up. Submissions already in flight cannot be recalled.
