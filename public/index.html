Your privacy, in plain words

  • New scans and queues are saved in this browser, including names, results, drafts, statuses and reminders. These records are not saved in our server database.
  • Use the same browser profile to return. Anyone using that profile can access these records. Clearing this site’s browser data removes them; private browsing may discard them when closed.
  • Live search sends queries through this site’s server only after your consent. External search engines may retain queries under their own policies. Source pages and images are not fetched. When you open a request for an unlisted site, only its hostname is sent to our server and to public DNS and domain-registration services to find an abuse contact. Result snippets come from search engines.
  • To enforce the public allowance, our server temporarily stores quota counters and a daily-changing keyed digest of your IP address. It does not store the raw IP address, queries or report records in these counters.
  • When required for a public scan or assistance request, Cloudflare Turnstile processes your IP address and browser signals to check for automated abuse. We do not send names, search queries or report details to the challenge widget.
  • Operator and partner sign-in use an essential session cookie. Credentials are not stored with browser reports.
  • Requests you choose to send through the contact form are emailed to the 123 Reputation Management team. Partner organisations are contacted only with your agreement.
  • AI drafting is optional: if you choose “Draft with AI”, the details of that one result are sent to our AI provider to write a draft. Nothing else is sent, and we don’t store it. Matching rules and request templates run in this browser.
  • You can delete individual records or clear all toolkit data below. Assistance requests share only the details you explicitly choose to submit.
