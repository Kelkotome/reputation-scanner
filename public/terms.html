Terms of use

The rules for using this free Reputation Scanner. By using it, you agree to these terms.

1. About the scanner

The Reputation Scanner is free, open-source software that helps you find online mentions of yourself or a business you represent, organise them, and prepare your own removal requests. It’s run by the operator of this website (“we”, “us”). It’s free to use, and we don’t take payment from users or from the partner organisations we refer people to.

2. Who can use it

  • You can search for yourself, or for a business you own or are authorised to represent.
  • Parents and guardians can search for a child in their care.
  • If you’re under 18, we encourage you to use the scanner with a parent, guardian or another adult you trust.

3. What you agree not to do

  • Search for, monitor or profile anyone else without their authority, or use the scanner to stalk, harass, threaten or expose anyone.
  • Send false, misleading or abusive removal requests, or claim rights you don’t have.
  • Upload or send intimate images, identity documents or passwords.
  • Get around the daily limits or bot checks, overload the service, or use automated tools against it.
  • Use the scanner for anything unlawful.

We may block access if we reasonably believe these terms are being broken.

4. Not legal advice, and no guarantees

  • The scanner and its request templates are general self-help tools. They aren’t legal advice, and using them doesn’t create a lawyer–client relationship.
  • Search results come from outside search engines and may be incomplete, out of date or about someone else with the same name. Matching and ranking are automated and can be wrong, so check every result.
  • Websites and platforms decide for themselves whether to remove anything. We can’t guarantee any outcome.
  • You’re responsible for reviewing, editing and sending every request yourself. Nothing is sent without your action.

5. AI drafting (if switched on)

If AI drafting is available, you can choose to have an AI service suggest a draft for one request at a time, after you confirm a consent screen. AI drafts can contain mistakes, for example misstating facts or describing a law or policy wrongly. Check and edit every draft before you send it.
