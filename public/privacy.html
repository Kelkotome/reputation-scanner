Reputation Scanner

Privacy notice

How this Reputation Scanner handles your information. Most of what you enter never leaves your browser.

The short version

  • Your scans, results, drafts, cases and reminders are saved in your own browser, not on our server.
  • A live search only runs after you agree to it. The search words, including the name you search, pass through our server to a search service and on to search engines. We don’t keep them.
  • To enforce the free daily limit, we keep a scrambled code made from your IP address that changes every day. We don’t store your IP address or what you searched.
  • We only receive your name, email and message if you choose to send a help request.
  • Optional features, such as sending email from your own account or AI drafting, only run if they’re switched on and you choose to use them.
  • We don’t use analytics or advertising cookies, and we don’t sell your information.

1. Who we are

This Reputation Scanner is free, open-source software. It’s run by the operator of this website. In this notice, “we”, “us” and “our” mean that operator. To contact us about privacy, use the contact form.

2. What stays in your browser

The scanner saves your work in your browser’s own storage (IndexedDB, plus a small amount of local storage). That includes:

  • the names you search, country or region, and any context you add, such as a city, employer, school or business details
  • the concerns you select, and whether the scan is for someone under 18
  • search results and snippets, and your labels such as “Not me”
  • removal request drafts, known-URL cases, sent dates, reference numbers and 14-day reminders

We can’t see, access or recover this information, and it doesn’t sync to other devices. Anyone who uses the same browser profile can open it. Clearing this site’s data, closing a private window, or your browser clearing storage on its own will remove it.

You can delete a single scan or case from its page, or use “Clear all toolkit data” on the home page. Deleting records in your browser doesn’t remove pages from other websites or withdraw requests you’ve already sent.

3. What leaves your browser, and why

3.1 Live searches (with your consent)

Before a live scan starts, you tick a box agreeing to share search queries. Your browser then sends each query to our server, which passes it to a search service and on to the search engines it uses. A query usually includes the name you entered. The results (titles, links and short snippets) come back to your browser and are saved there. Our server doesn’t store queries or results.

Search engines receive the query and may keep it under their own privacy policies. If a backup search service is switched on, the consent box names it before you agree. Our server refuses searches that contain intimate-content terms and points you to specialist support instead.

3.2 The free daily limit and abuse protection

Free use is limited to a few scans per IP address per day, and people on the same network share that allowance. To enforce it and stop abuse, our server turns your IP address into a keyed hash: a scrambled code made with a secret key and the day’s date. It changes every day, and we don’t store your raw IP address. We keep that code with a scan permit, short-term counters and short-lived locks. None of these records contain your name, searches or results.

3.3 Bot check

Before a public scan, a help request or an AI draft, you may be asked to complete a bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile). It processes your IP address and browser signals to detect automated abuse, under Cloudflare’s Turnstile privacy policy. We don’t pass your name, searches or messages to it.

3.4 Finding where to report a website

When you open a removal request for a website the scanner doesn’t already know, it looks up an abuse contact. Only the website’s hostname (for example, example.com) is sent to our server, which checks public DNS and domain registration records (RDAP). The full page address, your name and your draft aren’t sent. We don’t keep the result, and we never open the reported page or any images.

3.5 Help requests

If you send a help request through the contact form, we receive your name, email address, the type of help you want, your message and any links you add. If you also tick the box to attach your report, we receive the details shown in the preview. Please don’t send intimate images, identity documents or passwords.

Requests are emailed to us. After delivery, our server keeps only a short receipt (an ID, the date, the type of help and that it was delivered). If delivery fails, the full request is kept so it isn’t lost. Our server deletes these records after 30 days. We use your request only to reply and help you, and we pass it to a partner organisation only if you agree.

3.6 Sending email from your own account (if switched on)

If this feature is switched on, you can connect a Gmail or Outlook account to send requests you’ve reviewed. We ask only for permission to send email and to read your address, never to read your inbox. Access is stored encrypted on our server for up to an hour, or up to 30 days if you choose automatic follow-ups. Messages pass through our server when you confirm a send and aren’t kept afterwards. Disconnecting removes the stored access and cancels queued follow-ups.

3.7 AI drafting (if switched on)

If AI drafting is switched on, you can ask for an AI-written draft for one request at a time. It runs only after you confirm a consent screen, which names the AI provider. Only that one result’s title, link and snippet, the platform name, the request type and any facts you type (up to 2,000 characters) are sent to our server and the AI provider. Your name and email aren’t sent unless you type them in. Our server doesn’t store or log the prompt or the draft, and the AI provider handles the data under its own terms. AI drafts can contain mistakes, so check every draft before you send it.

3.8 Partner and staff sign-in

Partner organisations and staff can sign in to separate areas of the site. For partners we keep their work email, organisation name, daily allowance and a daily count of scans for up to 30 days. Staff sign-in uses an essential session cookie. Neither affects public users.

3.9 Usage totals

We keep daily totals for up to 30 days, such as the number of scans, searches and AI drafts and their estimated cost. They don’t contain names, queries, results or IP codes.

4. What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account, and the scanner is free.
  • We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don’t sell or rent your personal data, or use it for targeted advertising.
  • We don’t open the pages in your results, or fetch, upload or analyse images.
  • We don’t try to identify who posted something.

Our server is set up not to log search or form contents. Hosting and service providers still process requests and may keep technical records under their own policies.

5. Who else receives information

We share information only as this notice describes:

  • Our hosting provider runs the app and its database, and the bot check.
  • Search services and search engines receive live search queries.
  • Public DNS and domain registries receive the hostnames of websites you’re reporting.
  • Google or Microsoft receive messages you choose to send through a connected account, if that feature is on.
  • The AI provider named on the consent screen receives the details of a draft you choose to make, if that feature is on.
  • Our email provider delivers help requests to us.
  • Partner organisations, such as lawyers, PR firms, investigators and NGOs, only with your agreement. They handle your information under their own policies.
  • Authorities or others, if the law requires it or to protect someone’s safety.

These providers may process information in other countries.

6. How long we keep information

  • Browser records: until you delete them or your browser clears them. We have no copy.
  • Search queries and results: not stored on our server.
  • Daily IP codes, permits and abuse counters: from seconds to a few days, then deleted automatically.
  • Help requests: a receipt, or the full request if delivery failed, for up to 30 days on our server. Copies in our inbox are kept only as long as we need them to help you.
  • Email connections and follow-ups: up to 30 days.
  • Usage totals and partner counts: 30 days.

Our database provider may keep rolling backups for a limited period, so deleted records may stay in backups until they expire.

7. Children

Young people under 18 can use the scanner to search for themselves, and parents or guardians can search for a child in their care. When a scan involves someone under 18, the scanner shows safety and support resources first. If you’re under 18, we encourage you to involve a parent, guardian or another adult you trust. Cases involving intimate images of anyone under 18 should go to the police or a specialist service, and the scanner points you to them.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection law may give you the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent. To ask, use the contact form. We may need to confirm who you are first.

We can’t access or delete records in your browser, because only you control those. We usually can’t link a daily IP code back to you, and those records are deleted within days. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), keep secrets on the server only, encrypt stored email access, use keyed hashes instead of IP addresses, and delete short-term records automatically. No system is perfectly secure. Keep your browser profile private, because anyone using it can open your saved scans.

10. Open source

The scanner’s source code is public, so anyone can check how it handles data. If this notice and the code ever disagree, the code shows what actually happens.

11. Changes to this notice

We’ll update this notice when the scanner changes, and before we switch on any new feature that sends information off your device.
