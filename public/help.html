Support comes first

You do not have to
handle this alone.

For intimate-image abuse or child-safety concerns, specialist support is the right next step. The Reputation Scanner does not search for or analyse that content. For adult cases, a separate URL organiser can help you prepare a request from page addresses you already know.

If someone is in immediate danger

Contact your local emergency services. In Singapore, call the police on 999. For non-emergency reporting, visit the Singapore Police Force contact page.

For people under 18

Consider asking a trusted adult to help you decide what to do next. You can continue to the Reputation Scanner after reviewing these resources.

Images taken when under 18

Take It Down

NCMEC's service for nude, partially nude or sexually explicit images taken when the person was under 18, even if they are now an adult. Do not download or share images to use the service.

Visit Take It Down ↗

Adults can manage known URLs without uploading images

If the affected person was 18 or older when the content was created, you can organise known page addresses and review requests to send yourself. Source pages are never fetched or verified. The specialist support services above remain available.
