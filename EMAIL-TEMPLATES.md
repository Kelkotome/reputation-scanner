# Proposed takedown-request emails

Users choose the template that fits their situation, enter their contact email, replace the placeholders and review the statements before sending from their own email account. Each request goes to the relevant publisher or platform separately. Sending a request does not remove content or establish that it is false. If a platform requires a reporting form, use the same reviewed wording in that form. Do not invent a recipient address.

## 1. Inaccurate allegation — correction or removal

**Subject:** Request to review inaccurate information — [page title]

Hello,

I am contacting you about information concerning me on this page:
[Full URL]

The statement I dispute is: [Quote the specific statement].
I believe it is inaccurate because: [Explain the issue using facts you can support].
Supporting information: [Describe relevant evidence or provide a suitable reference].

Please review the statement and correct or remove it if your review confirms the issue. Please let me know how to provide supporting information privately and confirm the outcome of your review.

Thank you,
[Your name / authorised representative]
[Your contact email]

## 2. Private photo shared without consent — known URLs

**Subject:** Removal request: private image shared without my consent

Hello,

I am the person depicted in private imagery published at the following known URLs:
[Full URL(s)]

I did not consent to its publication or distribution at these locations. Please review this report under your non-consensual intimate-content or privacy policy and remove or disable access to the material.

Please confirm receipt, provide a case reference and let me know the outcome. If you need further information, please direct me to your secure reporting process. I have not attached the imagery to this email.

Thank you,
[Your name]
[Your contact email]

Use this adult template only when these statements are true. Authorised representatives should identify their role accurately. Under-18 or uncertain-age cases follow the app's specialist support workflow; do not request image attachments.

## 3. Exposed personal information — removal or redaction

**Subject:** Privacy request: remove or redact personal information

Hello,

This page contains personal information identifying me:
[Full URL]

Information to review: [Describe the type of information and where it appears, without unnecessarily repeating it].
Privacy concern: [Explain why it should not be publicly accessible].

Please review this under your privacy policy and remove or redact the identified information where appropriate. Please confirm receipt and tell me if any further information is needed through a secure channel.

Thank you,
[Your name]
[Your contact email]

## 4. Mistaken identity — clarification

**Subject:** Request to correct a mistaken identity — [page title]

Hello,

I believe this page incorrectly identifies me, or may cause confusion with another person who has a similar name:
[Full URL]

The relevant distinction is: [Explain the difference using appropriate, verifiable context].
Requested correction: [Describe the specific name, caption or statement to review].

Please review the identification and correct it or add a clarification where appropriate. Please tell me how to provide supporting information privately if needed.

Thank you,
[Your name / authorised business representative]
[Your contact email]

## 5. Follow-up on an existing request

**Subject:** Follow-up: removal/review request dated [date] — [reference]

Hello,

I am following up on the request I sent on [original sent date] about:
[Full URL(s)]

Case reference: [Reference, if supplied].
My original request concerned: [Brief factual summary].

Please confirm the current status and whether you need any further information from me. If this is not the appropriate reporting channel, please direct me to the correct one. I would appreciate confirmation of the review outcome when available.

Thank you,
[Your name]
[Your contact email]

## Proposed app presentation

- First ten priority results: title, domain, full URL and **Prepare takedown request**.
- Additional results: grey preview with no full URL; **Contact us to uncover more**.
- Draft dialog: eligible template choices, user's contact email, editable subject/body and an accuracy confirmation.
- Sending: open the user's email app only when a reporting email is known; otherwise use the platform's reporting form or copy the request to a verified destination.
- Tracking: **Request sent** is separate from **Removed**. Only record removal after the user checks the outcome.

The existing app already includes inaccurate-claim, personal-information and same-name templates, plus known-URL initial/follow-up requests. The wording above is a proposal for the next template refinement, not a claim that all five have been replaced in code.
